# Mint: business plan

Working plan for Mint, Practice Optimization Partners. Internal; not published on the site.

## Identity

- Brand is **Mint**. Descriptor is **Practice Optimization Partners**.
- Not an MSO. Avoided for legal complexity and the private-equity connotation.
- "Partners", not "consultants". Consultants sweep in and leave.
- Staffing is out of scope: it triggers MSO classification.

## Market

- Starts in Arizona, in the Phoenix area: Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Glendale, Tempe,
  Chandler, Gilbert, Goodyear and Peoria.
- Targets both solo practices and groups.

## Domain and branding

- Website: practiceoptimize.com. Email on the same domain.
- Buy a "mint" redirect as a backup (about $30 for both).
- The brand stays "Mint" on all materials; the URL is practiceoptimize.com.
- Rejected: mintpop, mintops, mint.ai, joinery, TrueCourse.
- Trademark: Ira to check "Mint" and "Mint Practice Optimization Partners". Closest

## Elevator pitch

> We're Mint. We're practice optimization partners. We start with a discovery period: a
> full evaluation of optimization opportunities in your practice. We deliver a playbook,
> then execute on it.

Three pillars: Operations, Revenue Opportunities, Marketing.

## Sales process

1. **Questionnaire.** Pre-meeting filter, not part of discovery. Doubles as qualification:
   a practice that won't fill it out isn't serious. Built per specialty, podiatry first.
   About 70 to 80% of questions are common across specialties.
2. **Discovery.** $1,500 flat. Deliverables: current state map and future state map, framed
   as "optimization opportunities". The visual before and after creates buy-in before any
   workflow changes. The fee rolls into the first month if the client signs on.
3. **Playbook, then execution.**

## Service areas

### Operations

- Phones and scheduling.
- Visit prep (e.g. orthotics at the front desk before the patient arrives).
- Collections: copay up front for insurance, cash pay at time of service. Don't let copays
  accumulate across visits.
- Documentation: AI-assisted EMR workflows.
- Patient follow-up: post-visit email summary plus a voicemail drop in the doctor's own
  voice. Warm and personal, no PHI in the voicemail.
- Workflow optimization: catch-all for process improvements.

### Revenue opportunities

- Census-based outreach to patients eligible for additional services.
- Goal: raise per-patient visit value.
- Model: Gecko-style census review.

### Marketing

- Remarketing to the existing patient base by email.
- Health newsletters: one topic per send. Two tracks, timely (illness alerts) and evergreen
  (condition-specific).
- Google reviews: no bare QR codes, since only angry patients self-select. Take the top 150
  loyal patients from the census and run a drip text campaign with a direct review link.
  Follow up after 3 to 5 days without a click; stop after about 4 reminders.
- Boots on the ground plus digital (SEO, Google Maps, social).
- Social: hyper-local quality over frequency. A monthly 3 to 5 minute video from the doctor
  beats generic holiday posts. AI search favors long-form, locally relevant content.
- ZocDoc: viable only with active staff management. Unmanaged leads show at 5 to 10%, and
  the practice pays per lead regardless. New patients must be called to confirm.

### Patient messaging and apps (separate service line)

- Best app use cases: activity tracking and secure messaging.
- Text check-ins (yes/no) replicate most of it without a download.
- Omnichannel inbox for inbound texts and emails.
