# Mint home page: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The client said "You have the
capabilities, now do it" and has not been interviewed against these eight questions.
Lines marked *client* are their words from the project conversation. Lines marked
*authored* are decisions made on their behalf and should be checked with them.

The skill's reference files, engine and scripts were not available in this session, and
there was no asset generation. The build uses three client-supplied photographs.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** *Client:* "more new age", "not a 2023 or 2024 website. This is 2026, 2027",
   "realistic images and realistic flow", "not like a cartoon". *Authored:* photographic,
   calm, botanical, exact. References: a macro nature film, a fragrance counter, a
   field guide.
2. **Scroll journey.** *Client:* "as you're going down the page ... more of the mint is
   sprouting, or a leaf is coming off, or it's growing", "play off the word mint ... but
   around practice optimization". *Authored sequence:* the promise, the day as it runs
   now, the same day fixed, where Mint works, the practice stays yours, the plant
   growing through the four steps, where Mint operates, the one action.
3. **Energy curve.** *Authored:* calm open, tightening through the problems, release at
   the wipe, steady through the pillars, a quiet band, then the most motion on the page
   in the growth scene, and a still close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** *Authored.* See the feeling curve and peak below.
5. **The one thing no other site does.** *Client:* the mint grows as you scroll.
   *Authored:* the visitor's scroll pulls focus as well as growing the plant. See
   signature move.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** *Authored:* editorial-photographic. Large serif
   type, real photographs, one accent. *Client constraint:* the 60-30-10 colour rule.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** *Authored:* distinct scenes that share one plant.
   Three photographs cannot carry a continuous flight.
8. **Assets.** *Client-supplied:* two studio photographs of mint sprigs (612 px stock
   previews) and one dark close-up (1024 px). Licence to be confirmed by the client.
   A logo designed during this project. No footage.

## What the visitor must believe

Mint will find what is holding the practice back and stay to fix it, and the practice
remains the doctor's own.

## The one action

"Start a conversation", the same label everywhere.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Calm confidence | A sharp sprig against deep, soft foliage; layers drift apart as the page moves |
| The day | Recognition turning to unease | Plain statements of the visitor's own problems, one after another |
| Pillars | Clarity | The page turns sideways: three panels pass like options on a shelf |
| Yours | Reassurance | The page goes still; one sentence over dark leaves |
| Growth (peak) | Momentum, a little wonder | The plant rises and comes into focus under the visitor's own scroll |
| Arizona | Grounded | A map and plain words, no motion |
| Close | Resolve | A still band of colour with one button, leaves settling |

**The peak.** "You scroll and the mint plant actually grows and comes into focus, step
by step." It lives in the growth act, which has the longest span on the page, and the
act before it is the stillest.

**Tell-someone sentence.** It's the site where your scrolling grows a real mint plant
and pulls it into focus.

**Authored silence.** The "Your practice stays yours" band is deliberately still.

## Grammar

Chaptered scenes: distinct acts that each behave differently, joined by one recurring
subject. Navigation is a plain sticky bar. The hero is a layered photographic scene.
The close is a still, full-colour band. Bans adopted for this build: no continuous
camera flight, no section counters, no scroll cue, no eyebrow above every heading, no
drawn illustration in the scroll scenes. The skill's eight defined grammars could not
be consulted, so this was chosen from the options named in its overview; a single
filmic flight was ruled out because there is no footage.

## Signature move

Focus pull on scroll. In the growth scene the visitor's scroll does two things at once:
the photographed sprig rises from its base, and the focal plane moves from the leaves
near the lens to the plant, so the foreground softens as the subject sharpens.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | Layered parallax, pinned | Depth is the strongest signal that this is a photograph |
| Tension and turn | Two static tables | Client direction: keep it simple. Today beside With Mint, no interaction |
| Range | Horizontal pan | Sideways travel reads as options |
| Reassurance | Static band | The stillness is the point, and it sets up the peak |
| Substance | Pin with scroll-driven growth and focus pull | The plant responds to the visitor's hand |
| Ground | Static split | Information, plainly |
| Commitment | Still band | The page stops and asks |

Five device families, none twice in a row. The client removed the hover list and the wipe scene in favour of two plain tables.
